## 11. SINGAPORE TAX & REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

### Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD)
- First S$180,000: 1%
- Next S$180,000: 2%
- Next S$640,000: 3%
- Next S$500,000: 4%
- Next S$1,500,000: 5%
- Amounts exceeding S$3,000,000: 6%

### Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD)
- **Singapore Citizens:** 0% on 1st residential property, 20% on 2nd, 30% on 3rd and subsequent.
- **Singapore Permanent Residents:** 5% on 1st residential property, 30% on 2nd, 35% on 3rd and subsequent.
- **Foreign Nationals:** 60% on any residential property.
- **Entities & Corporate Vehicles:** 65% upfront.
- **Free Trade Agreement (FTA) National Treatment Exception:** Nationals and Permanent Residents of Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland, and Nationals of the United States of America are accorded the same ABSD status as Singapore Citizens (0% on 1st residential acquisition).

### Foreign Ownership Restrictions (Landed vs. Non-Landed)
- **Landed Properties (Good Class Bungalows / GCBs):** Under the Residential Property Act, foreign nationals (including Permanent Residents) require express prior approval from the Land Dealings Approval Unit (LDAU) of the Singapore Land Authority. GCB approvals for non-citizens are extraordinarily rare and strictly contingent upon exceptional economic contributions.
- **Sentosa Cove Waterfront Landed:** The only designated exception in Singapore where foreign nationals may apply for expedited LDAU approval to acquire a 99-year leasehold landed property for owner-occupation.
- **Strata-Titled Properties (Super Penthouses, Duplexes, Condominiums):** Foreign nationals may purchase freely without prior LDAU approval.

### MAS Section 13O / 13U Family Office Structuring
Haute Terres Capital Advisory works alongside top-tier private banks (DBS, UBS, HSBC) to coordinate residential acquisitions within compliant Single Family Office (SFO) architectures, qualifying for tax exemptions under Sections 13O and 13U of the Income Tax Act.

## 16. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) & RAG RETRIEVAL PAIRS

### Q: Can non-Singapore citizens purchase landed Good Class Bungalows (GCBs)?
**A:** Under Singapore's Residential Property Act, foreign nationals (including Permanent Residents) require express approval from the Land Dealings Approval Unit (LDAU) of the Singapore Land Authority to acquire landed residential property. GCBs in prime mainland districts (such as Nassim Hill, Cluny Road, or Bukit Timah) are almost exclusively restricted to Singapore Citizens who have made exceptional economic contributions. However, foreign nationals can freely acquire ultra-luxury strata-titled properties, including super penthouses and Sentosa Cove oceanfront villas.

### Q: Are foreigners eligible to buy oceanfront villas in Sentosa Cove?
**A:** Yes. Sentosa Cove (District 04) is Singapore's only designated oceanic enclave where foreign buyers and non-residents can obtain LDAU approval to acquire 99-year leasehold landed waterfront villas and berths on an expedited basis.

### Q: What are the current Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) and Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) rates?
**A:** Standard BSD applies progressively up to 6% for residential properties above S$3,000,000. For ABSD: Singapore Citizens pay 0% on their first residential property, 20% on the second, and 30% on subsequent properties. Permanent Residents pay 5% on their first property and 30% on the second.
